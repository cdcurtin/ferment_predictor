Model: brix(t) = L + (U−L) / (1 + exp((t−t50)/s)), a 4-parameter logistic fit, one per ferment. Each fit is a MAP (maximum a posteriori) estimate combining that ferment's own readings with priors from 24 historical replicate curves (U = 25.7 ± 0.6, L = −1.45 ± 0.32, s = 1.18 ± 0.22, noise ≈ 0.68 Brix), so sparse early data shrinks toward known ferment shape and rate instead of overfitting. The midpoint (t50 = 3.43 d historically) is deliberately held loosely — its prior SD is widened to 2.5 d — so a ferment with a genuinely delayed onset can shift its whole predicted curve later once readings show the lag, rather than the model assuming typical timing regardless of evidence. Zero-Brix timing is only defined once a ferment's fitted plateau (L) is below zero — until then it reads "plateau > 0".
